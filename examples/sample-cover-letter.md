# Cover letter — Product Manager, Cartify (2024)

Dear Hiring Team,

When I read that Cartify wants to make checkout "invisible" for small merchants, I recognized a problem I have spent the last five years on.

At Marketly I owned checkout and payments. We ran more than 30 A/B tests in a year, and the one that mattered most was the simplest: removing a mandatory account step. Mobile conversion went up 9%. That taught me to look for the smallest change that removes the biggest doubt for the user.

At ShipFast I learned the merchant side. I interviewed more than 40 store owners to understand why returns were generating so many tickets, and the redesign we shipped cut them by 30%.

I would love to bring that mix of discovery and execution to Cartify. Thank you for your time.

Best regards,
Alex Morgan

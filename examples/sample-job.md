Cartify is an e-commerce platform for small merchants across Europe. We are looking for a Senior
Product Manager to own checkout and payments.

What you will do:
- Own the roadmap for checkout, payment methods and payouts.
- Work with engineering, risk and finance to launch new payment methods in three markets.
- Define and track conversion, authorization rate and payment cost metrics.

What we are looking for:
- 5+ years in product management, 2+ in payments or fintech.
- Experience with card payments, local payment methods and payment service providers.
- Strong analytical skills (SQL, Amplitude or similar).
- Fluent English; Spanish or Portuguese is a plus.

Hybrid in Lisbon or remote within Europe.
